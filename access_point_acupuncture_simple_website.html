Who this is for

Access Point Acupuncture is a good fit for people who want affordable, focused care and are comfortable with a simpler, streamlined treatment model.

A good fit for Access Point

  • Simple pain, stress, tension, or general wellness support
  • Maintenance-style acupuncture
  • Cost is an important consideration
  • You want shorter, straightforward visits

When comprehensive care may be better

If you need more in-depth evaluation, personalized planning, or ongoing support for a more complex case, NDCARE may be the better fit.

If you are unsure, just reach out. You can be guided to the best option for your needs.

How booking works

Email accesspointacu@gmail.com to request an appointment.
Include your main concern and whether you prefer a morning or afternoon time.
You will receive the next available options and booking instructions.
Once scheduled, you can bring cash to your appointment.

Simple payment model

To keep things simple and mission-focused, Access Point Acupuncture currently accepts cash. The sliding scale is $50–$90.

Frequently asked questions

How do I choose my sliding-scale amount? Choose the amount that best reflects your current financial situation. Those who can pay at the higher end help support access for others.
Is this the same as NDCARE? No. Access Point Acupuncture is a separate nonprofit program offering a more focused, affordable care model.
What if I need more comprehensive care? If a deeper level of support is needed, you can be guided to the most appropriate care model.
