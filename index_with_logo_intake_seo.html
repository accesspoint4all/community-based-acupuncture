What to Expect

Our Monday community clinic is designed to be simple, welcoming, and accessible for everyone.

  • You will be seen on a first-come, first-served basis within the clinic window.
  • Treatments are focused, effective, and tailored to your primary concern.
  • Sessions are typically shorter than private appointments but still provide meaningful support.
  • Please wear loose, comfortable clothing and come prepared to relax.
  • If this is your first visit, we will guide you through a brief intake before treatment.
  • No prior acupuncture experience is needed.
Reservation Policy
Payment reserves your spot for the Monday clinic and is applied to your visit. Missed visits without notice are non-refundable. Please notify us in advance to transfer your reservation.
