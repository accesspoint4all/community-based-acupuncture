Frequently Asked Questions

Is the $90 reservation for an exact appointment time?

No. It secures your place during the Monday clinic window from 12:00 PM – 2:30 PM. Patients are seen on a rolling basis.

Can I reserve the $70 rate online?

No. The $70 supported rate is available only for walk-in visits during Monday clinic hours and depends on availability.

Are walk-in visits guaranteed?

No. Walk-in visits are limited, first come, first served, and based on clinic capacity that day.

Are reservations refundable?

No. Reservations are non-refundable because payment secures a limited appointment space in the Monday clinic.

What forms of payment are accepted?

Online reservations are paid through the reservation link. For walk-ins, we accept cash and check during the clinic.
