How Access Point Acupuncture Works

We created two simple ways to receive care so the process stays clear, sustainable, and welcoming.

Option 1: Reserve Online

Reserve your spot in advance for $90. This is the sustaining rate and guarantees your appointment. It is ideal for those who want the convenience of a held space and a simple online process.

Option 2: Walk In

A limited number of walk-in spots are available on a $50–$70 sliding scale. Walk-ins are first come, first served and may require a short wait depending on the clinic flow that day.

Frequently Asked Questions

A few common questions to help you know what to expect.

What is community acupuncture?

Community acupuncture is a more accessible style of acupuncture offered in a shared treatment setting. It allows more people to receive care at a lower cost while still benefiting from a calm and supportive healing environment.

What does reserving online do?

Reserving online holds your appointment spot in advance at the $90 sustaining rate. This is the best choice if you want a guaranteed place.

Can I choose a lower price online?

No. Online reservations are only available at the $90 sustaining rate. Lower sliding scale rates are available only for limited walk-in spots.

Are reservations refundable?

No. Reservations are non-refundable because your payment is what holds the appointment space for you.

What if I cannot afford $90?

You are welcome to come as a walk-in and request one of the limited sliding scale spots. Walk-ins are first come, first served and may require a short wait.

What forms of payment are accepted?

Online reservations are paid through the reservation link. For walk-ins, cash and check are accepted.
